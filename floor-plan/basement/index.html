Things to check

  • Test the old floor tiles and black adhesive

    The dark square tiles still down in the walk-in closet and the black adhesive in both bedrooms can contain asbestos. Have a chip of each tested before any more steaming or scraping.

  • Climb-out windows

    A basement bedroom needs a window big enough and low enough to climb out of. Measure the opening (width and height) and the sill height of the bedroom and master windows. The bedroom's window opens under the deck.

  • The flue upstairs

    The furnace and the wall-hung water heater both vent through plastic pipe. If nothing else uses the metal flue in the chase beside bedroom 2, the chase can come out rather than be boxed in. Check what the metal pipe rising behind the dryer connects to.

  • Placed from photos

    The washer and dryer are on the east wall and the furnace and softener on the north wall, as you described; their exact spots along those walls, the back door, the windows and the bathroom rough-in are placed from the photos. The bathroom's walls are open framing, and it has no door yet; the opening is drawn where the photo shows a wider gap.
