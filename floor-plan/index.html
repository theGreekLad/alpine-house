Sizes are the measurements you gave, except the linen closet, which is estimated. Doors, windows and fixtures are placed from the walkthrough photos. On the drawing, a few rooms are stretched or shrunk by up to about 9 inches so the walls meet.

Things to check

  • Garage

    The garage is back to your measurement of 19′ 4″ deep. Its back wall lines up with the west side of the stairs, so it sticks out about 4′ 3″ past the front of the house.

  • Doors, windows and fixtures

    Every door, window and closet opening is drawn where the walkthrough photos show it, along with the kitchen cabinets, bath fixtures, front room duct and dining room baseboard heaters.

  • Window sizes

    Window widths are estimated from the photos. Measure them before ordering blinds or replacements.

  • Linen closet

    The linen closet at the south end of the hall is drawn at about 2′ 7″ × 2′. Send its real size if you measure it.

  • Flue chase

    The hatched space between the linen closet and bedroom 2 holds the flue pipe from the basement. It's the wall that's open in bedroom 2.

  • What lines up

    Across the bedroom side: green room 11′ 6″ + two 2′ closets + bedroom 2 at 9′ 6″ = 25′. Across the kitchen side: front room 11′ 6″ + stairs 3′ 7″ + kitchen 9′ 8″ ≈ 24′ 9″. Front to back, bedroom 2 + bath + kitchen comes to 28′ 1″ against 28′ 9″ on the front side. The 8″ difference is about two interior walls.
