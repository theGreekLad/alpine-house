What changes

  • The garage becomes a sunken living room, three steps down, with a vaulted ceiling and oak beams, a window seat where the garage door was, and a fireplace.
  • The kitchen moves to the old dining room by the deck, with a green island and stools. Dining takes the old kitchen spot, with glass doors to the deck.
  • The stair stays but opens up behind glass, with a skylight above it.
  • The front room becomes a foyer with a coat closet and bench, plus an office or guest room. A covered porch shelters the front door.
  • The two south bedrooms become one primary suite: the bedroom, a walk-in closet, and a spa bath with a freestanding tub and a walk-in shower.

About this model

  • It follows concept 6 on the layout concepts page, drawn on the measured plan. The outside walls and the stair stay where they are.
  • Colors and finishes are placeholders to show the feel: white oak floors, warm white walls, a soft green in the bedroom and a green kitchen island.
  • The sunken living room uses the garage floor's existing 1½–2 ft drop, and the vaulted ceiling assumes the garage rafters can be opened up. An engineer would confirm both.
  • The roof is left off, and the outside is kept simple.
