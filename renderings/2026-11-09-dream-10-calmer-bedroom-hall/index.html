About this model

  • They follow concepts 6 to 15 on the layout concepts page, drawn on the measured plan. The stair stays where it is, and the outside walls stay put except for the small entry addition in Dreams 2 to 4.
  • Colors and finishes are placeholders to show the feel: white oak floors, warm white walls, a soft green in the bedroom and a green kitchen island.
  • The sunken living rooms in Dreams 1 and 5 to 7 use the garage floor's existing 1½–2 ft drop, and their vaulted ceilings assume the garage rafters can be opened up. An engineer would confirm both.
  • The roof is left off, and the outside is kept simple.
